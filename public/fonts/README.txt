Self-hosted font resources for The Kitchen Codex.

Fraunces (roman + italic), Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono are
distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Sources:
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

The woff2 files here are the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts. They are
self-hosted so the production CSP (`default-src 'self'`) can load the
editorial and interface typefaces without a third-party font origin.
